\paragraph{Proposed FedQHD.}
In contrast, \textbf{FedQHD} targets \emph{structural heterogeneity}, where clients may use different encoders and different parameter dimensions, rendering parameter-space synchronization algebraically undefined.
FedQHD replaces parameter averaging with a \emph{function-space} federation primitive based on a shared anchor interface: client models are aggregated via Q-value consensus on anchor states, and the resulting global teacher is compiled back into each client’s private representation through a \emph{one-shot closed-form ridge or proximal-ridge projection}. This structure avoids iterative distillation, remains well-defined under mixed encoders and dimensions, and admits a homogeneous limit in which the method reduces exactly to FedAvg, providing a principled bridge between classical federated learning and heterogeneous federation.
